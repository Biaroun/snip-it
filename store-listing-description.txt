Snip It turns any web page into a source of notes. Highlight a sentence, a quote, a code snippet, a definition — right-click, and it's saved. No accounts, no cloud, no clutter. Just a fast way to capture what matters while you browse, and find it again later.

WHY SNIP IT

We take notes on the web constantly — copying quotes into random documents, screenshotting tweets, leaving fifteen tabs open "to remember." Snip It replaces all of that with one habit: select, right-click, save. Every note keeps a link back to where it came from, so you never lose the context.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Select any text on any web page.
2. Right-click and choose "Save as note."
3. Click the Snip It icon in your toolbar to see everything you've saved.

That's the whole workflow. No sign-up, no setup screen, no permissions beyond what the extension actually needs.

KEY FEATURES

- One-click capture — save selected text straight from the right-click menu, from any site.
- Automatic source tracking — each note keeps the page title and a link back to where you found it.
- Instant search — filter through all your notes as you type, across text, title and URL.
- Copy or delete in one click — hover any note to copy it to your clipboard or remove it.
- Markdown export — download your entire note collection as a single, clean .md file, ready for Obsidian, Notion, or any text editor.
- Bilingual interface — use Snip It in English or French, switchable anytime from the options page, independent of your browser's language.
- Fully local & private — every note is stored on your device with Chrome's built-in storage. Snip It has no backend server, sends no data anywhere, and includes no analytics or trackers.
- Lightweight — built with vanilla JavaScript and Manifest V3, no bloated frameworks, minimal permissions.

WHO IT'S FOR

- Researchers and students collecting quotes and references across many pages.
- Writers gathering material and sources for an article.
- Developers saving snippets, error messages, or documentation excerpts.
- Anyone who's tired of losing good ideas in a pile of open tabs.

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED

- contextMenus — adds the "Save as note" option to your right-click menu.
- storage — saves your notes and preferences locally on your device.
- downloads — lets you export your notes to a Markdown file when you choose to.

Snip It never requests access to the content of the pages you browse beyond the text you explicitly select and choose to save.
